4. BLE CONNECTION, POWER MANAGEMENT & WATCHDOG PARITY:
   - Responsive BLE Advertising Intervals (Zero-Reset Discovery): Fast pairing burst (FAST_ADV_BURST_WINDOW_MS = 300000UL) uses BLE_ADV_FAST_MIN/MAX_INTERVAL_UNITS = 96..160 (60-100 ms); ULP sleep advertising uses BLE_ADV_ULP_MIN/MAX_INTERVAL_UNITS = 320..480 (200-300 ms) so iOS CoreBluetooth discovers and connects within ~1-2s even after the 5-minute window expires without pressing RST.
   - Active-Link Stability & Timed Light Sleep: IDLE_LIGHT_SLEEP_DURATION_US = 100000ULL (100ms ticks) runs when disconnected (!deviceConnected), gated off during boot grace (5s), USB tethering, active BLE connections (deviceConnected), 5-minute fast pairing window (isInFastPairingWindow), pendingFastAdvRestart, and active tests (currentMode != MODE_IDLE). Idle BLE connections negotiate 150-300ms (120-240 units, slave_latency = 1, timeout = 6000ms) after the 5s GATT handshake to prevent 5-second ESP32-C6 XTAL/link-layer drops, switching to 30-60ms (24-48 units, slave_latency = 0) during active breath tests.
   - eFuse-Calibrated Battery ADC & Hardware USB SOF Detection (battery_monitor.h / .cpp): readPercentageAndVoltage() averages ADC_OVERSAMPLE_COUNT = 4 samples of analogReadMilliVolts(BATTERY_ADC_PIN) (ADC_RESOLUTION_BITS = 12, MILLIVOLTS_PER_VOLT = 1000.0f, VOLTAGE_DIVIDER_RATIO = 2.0f) and maps readings < MIN_PHYSICAL_CELL_DETECT_VOLTS (1.50f) to USB_POWERED_FALLBACK_PERCENT (100%). BatteryMonitor::isCharging() checks static_cast<bool>(Serial) OR live 1 ms hardware SOF increments on USB_SERIAL_JTAG.fram_num.val (USB_SOF_PROBE_DELAY_MS = 3UL, USB_SOF_HOLD_MS = 100UL). CRITICAL_LOW_BATTERY_SHUTDOWN_VOLTS = 3.10f with 3 consecutive checks (30s debounce), 1100ms BLE drain delay, and 60s timer wakeup (esp_sleep_enable_timer_wakeup(MesoPowerConfig::EMERGENCY_SLEEP_WAKEUP_US)).
   - iOS Connection Watchdog & Auto-Pair (BluetoothManager+CentralManager.swift): BMConstants.connectionTimeoutSeconds = 7.0s cancels stalled .connecting states and restarts scan; savedMesoNoseUUID == nil enables automatic first-time proximity pairing (BMConstants.proximityRSSIThreshold = -80 dBm).
   - iOS Peripheral Lookup & External Power Handling (BluetoothManager+MesoNose.swift): mesoNosePeripheral matches name prefix or savedMesoNoseUUID; BMConstants.zeroBatteryThreshold maps tethered 0% readings to tetheredFallbackBatteryPercent (100%) with isExternalPower = true.
